Candles = actual 5-minute prices (re-grouped as you zoom; weekends and the daily 1-hour break are left out). Purple line: over the last 5 trading days it shows, for each day, what the forecast predicted 24 trading hours earlier (compare it with the candles); from now on it is the forecast for the next 48 trading hours, drawn by the model in use (see Forecast model: every model is re-fitted daily and the one with the smallest past error is used). The darker band = 80% of that model's past errors, the lighter band = 95% (if enabled). Blue = best/2nd-best buy hours, orange = sell hours, picked from the typical intraday pattern of the chosen period; the small boxes are the price zones. Buy ≈ = the typical lowest price within that hour on similar past days (a limit order there filled on about half of days); zone = the middle half of those lows. Sell ≈ = the typical highest price within the hour; zone = the middle half of those highs. It is a statistical guide, not financial advice.
